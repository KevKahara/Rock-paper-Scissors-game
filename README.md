# Rock-paper-Scissors-game

    GAME OVERVIEW
RPS (Rock,Paper,Scissors) is a two party game whereby the parties involved 
choose one of the three options i.e either Rock, Paper or Scissors.

-Rock is signified with a closed fist
-Paper is signified with a fully opened palm
-Scissors is signified with the middle and index finger fully portrayed out like a real scissor

    GAME LOGIC
-Rock crushes Scissors
-Scissors cuts Paper
-Paper smothers Rock

    HOW IT IS PLAYED
1. There has to be two parties involved.
2. The parties have to choose either Rock or Paper or Scissors
3. Before either party throws their choice they have to count to 3 and simultaneously and immediately throw their choice
4.The one with the superior choice Wins the round
